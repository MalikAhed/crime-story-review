Sources & production credits

The painting that vanished is an illustrated historical account of the Mona Lisa theft and recovery. The visuals are original generated illustrations and stylized reconstructions, not archival footage or photographs of the events. Narration uses the creator’s authorized voice. Captions preserve the researched script; the preview is still awaiting final quality review.

Public-domain artwork is depicted within original illustrations. Modern museum photographs are not used as production footage. No invented dialogue, motive certainty or eyewitness testimony is presented.
